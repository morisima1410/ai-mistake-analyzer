ABOUT THE PLATFORM

About AI Mistake Analyzer

AI Mistake Analyzer is an AI-powered learning platform that helps students understand their mistakes instead of simply giving them the correct answer.

MISSION

Our Purpose

AI Mistake Analyzer is designed to help students learn from their mistakes. When a student submits a question and their answer, the system analyzes the response, identifies the type of mistake, explains what went wrong, provides the correct answer, and gives a personalized learning tip.

The goal is to make learning more understandable, interactive, and effective.
